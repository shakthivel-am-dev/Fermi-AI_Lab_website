First-principles meets Artificial Intelligence

Fermi-AI Lab is a research group in the Department of Physics and Nanotechnology, SRM Institute of Science and Technology, working on first-principles electronic structure research and materials informatics.

Our cause: the data-driven discovery of quantum and functional materials — faster, cheaper and more sustainable — while training the next generation of computational scientists.
